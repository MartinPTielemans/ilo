Keys beat clicks
On a Mac: ⌘+/ lists every key and Option+/ finds any menu command by its name. Ctrl+M adds a slide and ⌘+D duplicates the one selected. ⌘+Up and ⌘+Down move a slide in the filmstrip. ⌘+Option+Shift+S shows the speaker notes. ⌘+Enter presents from this slide and ⌘+Shift+Enter from the first. ⌘+Option+G groups and ⌘+Option+Shift+G ungroups.
Elsewhere Ctrl stands for ⌘ and Alt for Option, but a new slide is Ctrl+M too, and presenting is Ctrl+F5.
Presenting: the right and left arrows move a slide, a number then Enter goes to that slide, s shows the speaker notes and Esc stops.

Know before you start
With a shape selected, ⌘+Up and ⌘+Down bring it forward and send it back instead.
