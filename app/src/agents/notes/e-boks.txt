Go straight there
On e-Boks' mail site, /privat is a person's mailbox and /erhverv a business's.
On its information site, /danmark/da/kom-i-gang/ is how to start, /danmark/da/kontakt/ how to get in touch, and Brug for hjælp at the top leads to its help.

Know before you start
The user can also read their mail in e-Boks' app.
The site is in Danish, with English under En.
