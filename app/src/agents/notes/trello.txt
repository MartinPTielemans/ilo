Keys beat clicks
Shift+? lists every key. b finds a board by a few letters of its name. f opens a board's card filter and x clears it. j and k, or the arrows, select the card below or above. Over a card, t edits its title, n adds a card under it and c archives it. z undoes and Shift+Z redoes.

Know before you start
c archives the card under the pointer, so keep it off cards the user means to keep.
Undo can't bring back a deleted card, list or label.
The user can turn the keys off. If a key does nothing, click instead.
