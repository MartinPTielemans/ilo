Keys beat clicks
/ puts the cursor in the search box, and Shift+? lists every key.
On a video: k plays or pauses, j goes back 10 seconds and l forward 10, 0 goes to the start and 1 to 9 jump to 10% to 90% of it.
c turns captions on or off, m mutes, f fills the screen until f or Esc, > plays faster and < slower.
Paused, . steps one frame on and , one back.
Shift+N plays the next video, and Shift+P the one before in a playlist.

Know before you start
The player's keys may do nothing until the player has been clicked once.
The arrow keys, Home and End move through the video only while its seek bar has the keyboard.
