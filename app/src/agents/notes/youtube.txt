Keys beat clicks
/ puts the cursor in the search box, and Shift+? lists every key.
On a video: k plays or pauses, j goes back 10 seconds and l forward 10, 0 goes to the start and 1 to 9 jump to 10% to 90% of it.
c turns captions on or off, m mutes, f fills the screen until f or Esc, > plays faster and < slower, and i opens the miniplayer.
Paused, . steps one frame on and , one back.
Shift+N plays the next video, and Shift+P the one before in a playlist.

Know before you start
The player's keys work only once the player has been clicked.
On the seek bar, the left and right arrows move 5 seconds, up and down change the volume, and Home and End go to the start and the end.
