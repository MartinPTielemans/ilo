Go straight there
Search takes from:NAME, in:CHANNEL, mentions:NAME and has:, and its menu offers dates before and after.

Keys beat clicks
⌘+K on a Mac, Ctrl+K elsewhere, opens the quick switcher. ⌘+/ or Ctrl+/ lists every key.

Know before you start
Keys work in a browser, but keys the user made their own work only in Discord's app.
