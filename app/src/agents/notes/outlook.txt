Go straight there
Search takes from:NAME, to:NAME, subject:WORD, HasAttachment:yes and received:MM/DD/YYYY, two dots between two dates for the days between, and words such as today or last week. Quotes find a phrase, AND and OR join terms, a minus before one leaves it out, and no space follows a colon.

Keys beat clicks
With Outlook's own keys, Shift+? lists them, Alt+Q searches, n writes a new message, r replies, Shift+R replies to all and Shift+F forwards. e archives, q marks read and u unread, and Insert flags. Ctrl+Alt+1 opens Mail and Ctrl+Alt+2 the Calendar.

Know before you start
The user picks Outlook's, Gmail's or Yahoo Mail's keys, or none, under Settings, General, Accessibility. If a key does nothing or something else, click instead, and leave the setting as it is.
Shift+Delete deletes for good.
