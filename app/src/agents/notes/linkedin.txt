/messaging/ holds the user's messages.
Search's results have a tab a kind at the top: People, Jobs, Posts, Companies, Groups, Events and more. Once one is picked, All filters at the upper right narrows it further.
Search takes AND, OR and NOT in capitals, quotes for a phrase and parentheses to group, such as VP NOT (assistant OR SVP). It takes no brackets, braces or wildcards, and + and - may not work.
