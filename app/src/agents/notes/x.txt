Go straight there
Search takes from:NAME, to:NAME, since:2026-01-31 and until:2026-02-28, min_faves:10, min_replies:100, filter:media and filter:replies, quotes for a phrase, OR for either and a minus to leave a word out.

Keys beat clicks
? lists every key and / puts the cursor in search. n writes a new post. On a post, l likes it, r replies, t reposts and Enter opens it. g then h goes home, g then n to the notifications, g then p to the profile, g then m to the messages and g then s to the settings.

Know before you start
l and t act at once, where others see it, so press them only when the user asked.
