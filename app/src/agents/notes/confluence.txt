Keys beat clicks
Viewing a page, Shift+? lists the keys, and editing one, ⌘+/. e edits the page. Ctrl+Option+Z searches, ⌘+Option+H goes home and ⌘+Option+O opens the notifications.
Editing, / inserts anything, @ mentions someone, ⌘+K adds a link, ⌘+Option+1 makes a heading, ⌘+Shift+I a table and ⌘+Option+C a comment. ⌘+Enter publishes.
At a line's start, # and a space make a heading, * and a space a list, and [] and a space an action item.
Elsewhere Ctrl stands for ⌘ and Alt for Option, but search is Alt+Shift+Z.
