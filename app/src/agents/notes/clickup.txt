Keys beat clicks
⌘+K on a Mac, Ctrl+K elsewhere, searches everything. Shift+? lists the keys. s searches too, t makes a task and r a reminder.
In the editor, / lists commands, and @ mentions a person, @@ a task and @@@ a doc.

Know before you start
ClickUp's single keys are off until the user turns them on in their settings. If one does nothing, use ⌘+K or click instead, and leave the setting as it is.
Keys work only with the cursor outside a text field.
