Keys beat clicks
⌘+/ on a Mac, Ctrl+/ elsewhere, lists every key, and Ctrl stands for ⌘ in the rest. ⌘+K switches to another base and ⌘+J to another table. ⌘+F finds in the view, and ⌘+Shift+F opens its filters.
Space expands the record selected, and Esc goes back to the table. Enter or F2 edits the cell. Shift+Enter adds a record under it. Shift and the arrows select cells, and ⌘+Shift and the arrows select to the table's edge.

Know before you start
With the cursor in a cell or a field, ⌘+F may search that instead: click the grid first.
