Go straight there
Responses at the top of a form shows its answers: Summary for them all, once there is one, and Individual for one person's, with Previous and Next. Link to Sheets at the top right puts them in a spreadsheet, and More, then Download responses (.csv), gives a file.

Know before you start
A downloaded file isn't sorted by time.
With more than 10,000 responses, the views by question and by person may be missing.
