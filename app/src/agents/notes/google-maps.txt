Go straight there
/maps/search/?api=1&query=WORDS searches for a place or an address.
/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=A&destination=B shows the way, and travelmode= takes driving, walking, bicycling, two-wheeler or transit.
/maps/@?api=1&map_action=map&center=LAT%2CLNG&zoom=12 shows the map there.
Write a comma as %2C and a space as +. Keep ?api=1 in each, or Maps ignores the rest.

Keys beat clicks
Ctrl+/ lists the keys. With the map focused, the arrows move it, a square at a time with Shift, and + and - zoom.
