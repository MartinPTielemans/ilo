Go straight there
The search box opens the page whose title it matches exactly. A ~ anywhere in its words, or /w/index.php?title=Special:Search&search=WORDS&fulltext=Search, lists results instead.
Search takes intitle:WORDS, incategory:NAME with _ for each space, insource:WORDS for the page's source, hastemplate:NAME, linksto:PAGE, and prefix: as its last part. Quotes find a phrase, a minus or a ! leaves a word out, * and \? are wildcards, and a ~ after a word also finds spellings near it.

Keys beat clicks
Access keys, held with Ctrl+Option on a Mac and Alt+Shift elsewhere: f the search box, x a random article, h the history, t the talk page and z the Main Page.

Know before you start
In the editor, s publishes the edit for everyone at once, so press it only when the user asked.
