Keys beat clicks
/ opens search, which finds issues, projects and documents by ID, title, description or comments. An ID such as LIN-123 finds that issue, and words in quotes an exact term. o then i searches issue titles alone.
c makes an issue, and Option+C, Alt+C elsewhere, one from a template. g then i opens the inbox and g then m My issues.
In a list, j and k or the arrows move, x selects, and f opens the filters, where typing a status, a person or a team finds it.
In the inbox, u marks read or unread, h snoozes and Backspace deletes.
In the command menu, i and a space shows issues alone, p and a space projects, and d and a space documents.

Know before you start
⌘+K, Ctrl+K elsewhere, on selected issues lists what can be done to them all at once.
