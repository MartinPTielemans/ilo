Keys beat clicks
⌘ on a Mac stands for Ctrl elsewhere. ⌘+P or ⌘+K searches the workspace or jumps to a page seen lately, and ⌘+F searches the page. ⌘+N makes a page. ⌘+[ goes back and ⌘+] forward. ⌘+L copies the page's address.
In a page, / opens the menu of every kind of block. At a line's start, # and a space make a heading, - and a space a bulleted list, [] and a space a to-do, and > and a space a toggle. ⌘+D duplicates the blocks selected, ⌘+Shift and the arrows move them, and ⌘+/ turns them into another kind. ⌘+Shift+M comments, and @ and a name mentions someone.
