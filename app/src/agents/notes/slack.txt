Go straight there
Search takes in:CHANNEL or in:NAME, from:NAME, with:NAME, before:, after: and on: a date, during: a month or a year, has:pin, is:saved and is:thread. Quotes find a phrase, a minus leaves out a word or an in: or from:, and a * after three letters or more finds words starting with them.

Keys beat clicks
⌘ on a Mac stands for Ctrl elsewhere. ⌘+/ lists every key. ⌘+G opens search and ⌘+F searches the conversation open. ⌘+Shift+A opens All unreads. Option+Shift+Down and Option+Shift+Up, Alt elsewhere, go to the next and the last unread conversation. Esc marks the one open read. ⌘+N writes a new message. Up in an empty message box edits the last one sent.
Off a Mac, Ctrl+K jumps to a conversation by its name.

Know before you start
Shift+Esc marks every conversation read, so press it only when the user asked.
Threads, Activity and uploading have keys only in Slack's own app, not in a browser.
