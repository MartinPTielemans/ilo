Go straight there
/borger is for people and /erhverv for businesses.
/borger/aarsopgoerelse is the annual statement, årsopgørelsen, and /borger/aarsopgoerelse/betal-restskat/betal-restskat how to pay what's owed. /borger/forskudsopgoerelse is the preliminary assessment, forskudsopgørelsen, and /borger/forskudsopgoerelse/tjek-og-ret-forskudsopgoerelsen how to check and correct it.
/borger/fradrag is deductions, /borger/saadan-betaler-du-til-os how to pay, /borger/hovedkort-bikort-og-frikort tax cards, /det-mange-ringer-om what many call about and /kontakt how to get in touch.

Know before you start
The site is in Danish. Søg at the top opens its search, which asks that nothing personal, such as a CPR number, is typed in it.
